Descriptions of Result Plots by Filename and TCanvas Handle
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Handle	Filename Pattern	Description
--------------------------------------------------
c1	*_bursts		scintillation bursts -- interevent times, raw & quenched energies
c2	*_nu-trg		neutrino-trigger results
c3	*_pd-xyz		{x,y,z} coordinates of IBD-candidate prompt & delayed events
c4	*_results-ang-separate	reconstructed azimuthal & polar angles pointing to direction of incoming antineutrino,
                                    relative to a vertical axis with its origin at the center of the detector
c5	*_results-cos-psi	cos[psi] distribution comparing reconstructed vs. actual incoming antineutrino direction
c6	*_results-skymap	skymap of reconstructed incoming antineutrino directions; unless otherwise noted, true direction = (0°,0°)
c7	*_cap-prod		pie chart of neutron-capture product nuclei
